Lessons Learned the Hard Way

More than a club.

A workshop where students own the engineering.

Engineering, end-to-end

Students design in CAD, machine and 3D-print parts, wire electronics, and write the autonomous code. Mentors coach and help out where needed.

Compete & iterate

Each FTC season is roughly six months of design, build, code, and competition. We treat every meet as a data point — what failed, what held up, what we'd redesign tomorrow.

Every season, a new machine. From scratch.

How we build.

The game changes every September. Subject to a provided set of constraints, our students design a fresh robot every year — drivetrain, lift, intake, vision, control, and code, all chosen to suit that season's challenge.

  • DesignOnshape · CAD-first
  • FabricationStock parts · 3D printing · machining
  • DrivetrainMecanum or tank, season-dependent
  • ControlREV Control + Expansion Hub
  • VisionLimelight · AprilTag · OpenCV
  • CodeJava · Blocks · FTC SDK

Eight months. Same arc, every year.

Aug
Build the Team
School starts in mid-August. This is the time when we recruit new members and confirm returning ones.
Sep
Kickoff & Strategy
FIRST drops the new game on the first Saturday in early September. We watch the reveal as a team, read the manual cover-to-cover, search the internet for ideas and argue about scoring strategies for two weeks. We also create trial mechanisms to help us to design difficult features.
Oct
Design, Build, & Test
Mechanical design is done on the computer where it is easier using CAD. Parts are ordered as soon as possible before they sell out. Students assemble and wire the robot. Software design is started, but can't be tested until the hardware is built.
Nov
Begin Meet Competition
At this early point in the season, the robot is likely still not complete. Early competition give us the opportunity to learn from other teams and test our robot in real world conditions.
Dec
Compete & Modify
We continue to compete with other teams in Meets. We modify the design to improve our results. Beware of the trap of changing the hardware so much that operator training and software can't keep up.
Jan
More Meets & League Final
Finish tuning the robot. Prepare for the big event -- League Championship. Drive practice. Pit repairs. Sleep optional.
Feb - Mar
Post Season
If we place high enough in the League Championship, we can move on to the Regional Qualifier or State Championship. Placing high enough at State earns a spot at the World Championship in Houston.
Open to New Members

No experience required. Just curiosity.

Middle and high schoolers welcome. We'll teach you CAD, code, machining, electronics, fundraising, outreach — whatever pulls you in. Recruiting timing varies by year, so reach out anytime to learn how to join.
