2015-16 · RES-Q.

A search-and-rescue-themed game. Team 9960's rookie season, with mountain climbing, debris clearing, and zip-line endgames.

The RES-Q kickoff animation.

FIRST revealed RES-Q on September 5, 2015. Robots score by pushing the correct button on a beacon, dropping "climbers" into the correct boxes, collecting "debris" and dropping that into correct boxes and by climbing a steep mountain. The highest number of points can be earned if the robot can hang from a bar at the top of the mountain during the end game.

We experimented early on with many ways to allow the robot to climb the steep mountain. In an early meet, we saw a very clever hook design that could be extended and retracted. We implemented our version and greatly improved our scores in later meets.

How we did.

Result details are not available (FTC records only go back as far as 2019). Our robot could climb well and trip climbers, but could not sense color or push the beacon buttons. It also could sweep debris into the floor location, but not deposit it in mountain buckets.
