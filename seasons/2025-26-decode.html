2025–26 · DECODE.

An archaeology-themed shooting game. Color-coded artifacts, sequenced scoring, and an endgame climb. Our eleventh competitive season.

The DECODE kickoff animation.

FIRST revealed DECODE on September 6, 2025. Robots scored by collecting color-coded artifacts from the field and launching them at scoring goals — bonus points came from completing color sequences. The endgame asked robots to stop within a 18 inch parking area, with extra points if both alliance robots both occupy the same place.

We decided early on that the robot would have artifacts loaded by the human player and not picked up from the floor. The emphasis was on allowing the robot to shoot them in any order to score color sequence points.

Our 2025–26 machine.

Noteworthy Features

  • Drivetrain96mm GoBilda Mecanum, 4 vertically mounted motors
  • Ball CarriageThree cup, human-player loaded, can be shot in any order
  • Ball Launch3D printed channel, Gobilda Hogback wheel launcher
  • VisionLimelight 3A · AprilTag
  • NavigationOdometry using 2 deadwheels and an IMU
  • ElevatorTwo stage worm drive with steel feet, able to lift robot 18 inches off the floor
  • CodeJava for Autonomous, Blocks for Teleop

Reflections

What we learned.

At the league and state competition level, we saw robots that could load from the floor and still shoot artifacts in any order. However, robots that scored best did't worry too much about sequence but emphasized shooting as many artifacts as possible.

Human player loading was more difficult than we had anticipated, given that the pickup area was diagonally located on the floor from the target.

Rules this year allowed a robot to hit another one and/or block its ability to shoot. We deplore this sort of competition and hope next year's rules will be more in keeping with Gracious Professionalism.
